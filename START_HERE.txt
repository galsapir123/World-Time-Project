⚡ KEY FEATURES EXPLAINED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🔄 AUTO-REFRESH (60 Seconds)
   The app automatically updates all times every 60 seconds to keep them
   current. No manual action needed!

🌙 DAY/NIGHT INDICATORS
   • ☀️ Sun emoji appears for 6 AM to 6 PM
   • 🌙 Moon emoji appears for 6 PM to 6 AM
   Helps you understand what time of day it is in each city.

⏱️  MANUAL REFRESH
   On the city details page, click the 🔄 Refresh button to update that
   city's time immediately.

📊 REAL-TIME DATA
   All time data comes directly from the time.now Developer API, ensuring
   accuracy across all timezones.
